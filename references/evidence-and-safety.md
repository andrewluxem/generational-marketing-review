# Evidence and Safety

## Claim labels

Use these exact labels when useful:

- **Provided fact:** Explicitly supplied by the user.
- **Observed feature:** Directly visible in the plan or asset.
- **Inference:** Reasoned from supplied material.
- **Assumption:** Necessary but unverified.
- **Recommendation:** Proposed action.
- **Unknown:** Required information is missing.

## Evidence checks

For a quantitative or behavioral claim, look for:

- source;
- publication date;
- fieldwork date;
- geography;
- sample;
- methodology;
- category relevance;
- first-party confirmation.

Without these, do not present the claim as current fact.

## Prohibited behavior

Do not:

- recommend excluding or disadvantaging protected groups;
- infer sensitive traits from age or creative appearance;
- use dark patterns or deceptive urgency;
- advise evading platform disclosures;
- recommend collecting unnecessary personal data;
- present stereotypes as evidence;
- redistribute copyrighted source assets;
- expose private or confidential inputs.

## Safer transformation

Convert broad demographic claims into:

- a question;
- a hypothesis;
- a test;
- a segment definition;
- a research need;
- a confidence statement.
