# Marketing-Asset Review

## Review the asset itself

Assess:

- first impression;
- message hierarchy;
- value proposition;
- proof;
- offer clarity;
- call to action;
- tone;
- representation;
- readability;
- accessibility;
- platform fit;
- brand fit;
- purchase or next-step friction.

## Review the surrounding experience

An asset cannot be judged in isolation when the destination, offer, or fulfillment experience determines success.

Check:

- landing page or product page;
- mobile behavior;
- checkout;
- returns;
- delivery;
- support;
- consent and privacy;
- creator disclosure;
- measurement.

## Image and video assets

Check:

- visual hierarchy;
- readable text;
- captions;
- contrast;
- safe areas;
- pacing;
- authenticity and manipulation;
- representation;
- whether the product is clear;
- whether the asset relies on a stereotype.

## Copy assets

Check:

- clarity;
- specificity;
- credibility;
- information density;
- jargon;
- condescension;
- forced slang;
- unsupported claims;
- CTA alignment.

## Output

Name the exact element that should change. Avoid generic advice such as “make it more authentic” unless you define what that means operationally.
