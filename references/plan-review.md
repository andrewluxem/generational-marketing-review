# Marketing-Plan Review

## 1. Parse the plan

Extract:

- business objective;
- customer objective;
- audience definition;
- customer state;
- proposition;
- offer;
- channel roles;
- content formats;
- journey;
- budget or constraints;
- measurement;
- evidence.

## 2. Find contradictions

Examples:

- broad awareness objective with only conversion creative;
- trust-sensitive product with weak proof;
- social-discovery strategy with a difficult mobile checkout;
- purpose positioning without substantiation;
- creator strategy based only on reach;
- promotion strategy without margin or loyalty guardrails.

## 3. Apply the rubric

Score every dimension and identify critical overrides.

## 4. Apply the audience lens

Use `generation-lenses.md` as questions to ask. Do not give points merely because the plan mentions a platform, trend, creator, coupon, or nostalgic reference.

## 5. Create the change plan

Prioritize changes by:

- customer impact;
- business impact;
- evidence gap;
- execution effort;
- reversibility;
- risk.

## 6. Create the test plan

Every important generational assumption should become one of:

- verified fact;
- first-party analysis;
- qualitative research question;
- experiment;
- unresolved uncertainty.
