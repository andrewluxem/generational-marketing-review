# Review Rubric

Score every dimension from 0 to 4.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Missing, harmful, or directly contradictory |
| 1 | Weak and mostly unsupported |
| 2 | Mixed or incomplete |
| 3 | Strong with minor gaps |
| 4 | Strong, evidenced, and execution-ready |

## Dimensions

### 1. Audience definition

Check whether the cohort, birth range or life stage, geography, category, and customer state are clear.

### 2. Customer need and context

Check whether the plan solves a real need in the correct moment rather than relying on age alone.

### 3. Value proposition

Check clarity, relevance, differentiation, credibility, and expected customer benefit.

### 4. Message and tone

Check language, cultural relevance, respect, readability, and brand fit.

### 5. Creative and format

Check visual hierarchy, pacing, platform-native execution, information density, and production quality.

### 6. Channel and platform fit

Check whether the channel role is justified by evidence, objective, and customer behavior.

### 7. Trust and proof

Check reviews, demonstrations, comparisons, substantiation, transparency, policies, creator credibility, and social proof.

### 8. Offer and purchase friction

Check price communication, discounts, bundles, checkout, payment, and conversion friction.

### 9. Customer experience and fulfillment

Check delivery, returns, support, onboarding, loyalty, and post-purchase expectations.

### 10. Inclusion, accessibility, privacy, and ethics

Check representation, contrast, captions, readability, consent, data use, discriminatory targeting, and manipulative practices.

### 11. Measurement and experimentation

Check objective, primary metric, guardrails, baseline, test design, sample, and decision rule.

### 12. Evidence quality

Check source, recency, geography, methodology, first-party relevance, and whether assumptions are labeled.

## Critical override

A serious problem in accessibility, privacy, legality, deception, discrimination, trust, or evidence can override the average score.
