# Scenario Input: Loopwell Sling Gen Z Paid-Social Carousel

> Fictional company, fictional campaign. Contains no real statistics, no copied
> source language, no real campaign material, and no confidential information.
> All names, figures, handles, and terms are invented for this evaluation.

You are given the following draft paid-social carousel to review.

---

## Company and category

**Loopwell** is a US direct-to-consumer brand that sells one accessory for
reusable water bottles: the **Loopwell Sling**. The Sling is a universal
crossbody strap with a silicone grip cradle that clips onto most standard
wide- and narrow-mouth bottles, plus a small zip pocket sized for a card, key,
or transit pass. It is an add-on for a bottle the customer already owns; the
brand does not sell bottles. Everything is sold and shipped from
loopwell-example.com.

## Geography and language

United States only. English-language creative and site.

## Target audience

Intended audience: **US Gen Z adults, explicitly ages 18–29**. These are
first-time prospects. The brand also markets the Sling as an everyday carry
accessory for commuters, students, gym-goers, and festival-goers, but the paid
campaign audience for this asset is defined as Gen Z adults 18–29.

## Customer state

Every person reached is a **new prospect**: they have never visited
loopwell-example.com, never bought anything from Loopwell, and are not on any
Loopwell list. They are cold paid-social traffic seeing the brand for the first
time in an Instagram feed.

## Business objective

**Acquire first-time purchasers.** The goal of this campaign is a completed
first purchase from a new customer, not awareness, list growth, or engagement
for its own sake.

## Customer objective (as understood by the team)

Let a first-time viewer quickly decide **whether the Sling solves a real
everyday problem for them — carrying a bottle hands-free — and whether it is
worth $34.**

## Channel, placement, and device

- **Channel:** a single paid **Instagram carousel** ad. No other channel or
  placement is used in this campaign.
- **Placement:** mobile Instagram feed.
- **Device:** phones. The asset is designed for and delivered to the in-feed
  mobile experience.

## Offer, pricing, shipping, and returns

- **Price:** the Loopwell Sling is **$34**.
- **Incentive:** **15% off the first order** for new customers (first order
  only). No larger discount is offered.
- **Shipping:** **free shipping** on every order; orders **ship within 2
  business days** from a US warehouse and typically **arrive in 3–6 business
  days**. Shipping timing is stated plainly on the asset.
- **Returns:** **30-day returns, no questions asked**, with a prepaid return
  label included in the box. The return policy is stated plainly on the asset.

## Constraints

- **Five carousel cards. One caption. One landing-page destination.**
- **No customer testimonials, named reviews, or fabricated ratings.**
- **No discount larger than 15%.**
- **No targeting or creative directed at minors.**
- **Accessibility requirements apply** to the creative and the destination.
- US regulatory context applies, including truthful-advertising expectations
  and paid-partnership disclosure for creator content (FTC).

## Conversion event

The single defined conversion event is a **completed first purchase at
loopwell-example.com**, tracked as one purchase event in analytics.

## Targeting assumptions (as written in the media plan)

- **Channel assumption:** "Nearly all Gen Z discover new products through
  Instagram, so Instagram alone is enough to reach this audience." No source,
  first-party data, or test is attached to this claim, and no other channel or
  placement is considered.
- **Audience line (as written in the media plan):** "Target **all Gen Z
  users** on Instagram — we don't want to over-narrow the audience." The current
  draft ad set's age range is set to **13–65+**, not 18–29.
- The plan does not otherwise restrict the audience by age, and no age exclusion
  is configured beyond the broad "Gen Z" label.

## Creative strategy and hook (as written in the plan)

- **Hook rationale:** "Gen Z is obsessed with authenticity, so the whole
  carousel has to feel authentic. Card 1 leads with the most authentic bottle
  strap on your FYP." Authenticity is treated as a settled fact about the
  generation and as the campaign's central creative bet.
- **Voice:** the plan directs writers to "sound native to Gen Z" by leaning
  heavily on current slang across the headlines, overlays, and caption.

## Creator / UGC strategy (as written in the plan)

- The plan will seed the campaign with **six creators**, selected **mainly by
  follower count (250k+ followers) and whether their grid matches the brand's
  aesthetic**. There is no vetting for audience fit, engagement quality,
  comment sentiment, brand-values alignment, or whether the creator's audience
  skews to minors.
- Creator posts will reuse the carousel's copy and hashtags. The plan does not
  specify a paid-partnership disclosure.

## The carousel artifact

Five cards, one caption, one CTA, one destination. Draft copy is verbatim below.

### Card 1 — Hook

- **Visual:** a close-up of a bottle being slung crossbody as someone walks,
  shot to look like unpolished phone footage.
- **Headline (overlay):** "the most authentic bottle strap on your FYP fr"
- **Overlay copy:** "no cap this hits different bestie 💅 it's giving hydration,
  it's giving main character energy — you're gonna be lowkey obsessed, periodt"

### Card 2 — Options

- **Visual:** three Slings shown side by side for narrow-mouth, wide-mouth, and
  large sport bottles.
- **Headline (overlay):** "pick your fit bestie"
- **Overlay copy:** "which one's giving you? ⬅️➡️"
- **Note:** the **recommended, best-value option is indicated only by a green
  highlight ring** drawn around the middle Sling. There is no label, text, star,
  badge, or icon marking it as recommended — the green ring is the only signal.

### Card 3 — How it works

- **Visual:** a short in-feed video loop that clearly demonstrates the product:
  the strap clips onto a standard bottle, the wearer slings it crossbody
  hands-free, then drops a phone and a key into the zip pocket. Each step is
  shown literally, in order, in about three seconds.
- **Headline (overlay):** "clips on in 2 seconds"
- **Overlay copy:** "any standard bottle. hands free. pocket for your card + key."

### Card 4 — Details

- **Visual:** a lifestyle photo of a crowded festival, with a large block of
  overlay text set on top of the busy image.
- **Headline (overlay):** "everything you need to know"
- **Overlay copy (a dense block of text over the photo):** "universal fit for
  narrow + wide mouth bottles • adjustable strap 20–48 in • silicone grip
  cradle • zip pocket fits card key transit pass • sweatproof + rinsable •
  $34 with 15% off your first order • free shipping, ships in 2 business days,
  arrives 3–6 business days • 30-day returns no questions asked, prepaid label
  in the box • #hydration #everydaycarry #festivaltok #fyp #loopwell"

### Card 5 — Sustainability + CTA

- **Visual:** a sunrise nature shot.
- **Headline (overlay):** "sustainable by design 🌱"
- **Overlay copy:** "ditch single-use — the planet will thank you. tap to cop
  yours before they're gone."
- **Note:** no material, recycled-content, lifecycle, durability, or
  certification detail supports the sustainability line.

### Caption (one caption for the whole carousel)

> you needed 😭 no cap it hits different — clip it on any standard bottle, sling
> it crossbody, drop your card + key in the pocket, and you're OUT. it's giving

> shipping (ships in 2 biz days), 30-day returns no questions asked. don't be
> delulu, tap shop now before they sell out 💅 #loopwell #everydaycarry #fyp

### CTA and destination

- **CTA button:** "Shop now"
- **Destination:** the CTA links to the **Loopwell homepage
  (loopwell-example.com)**, not to a product-specific page for the Sling. A
  first-time viewer lands on the general homepage and must find the Sling
  themselves.

### Purchase path (mobile)

- On mobile, the checkout **requires creating an account (email + password)
  before a first purchase can be completed**. There is **no guest checkout**.
  A new prospect must register an account before they can buy.

## Measurement plan (as written)

- **Primary success metrics:** **impressions, reach, and video views**
  (3-second plays). These are the numbers the plan will report as success.
- **Secondary metrics:** saves and shares.
- There is **no holdout or control group**, so the campaign cannot show whether
  it drove purchases that would not have happened anyway.
- There is **no incrementality or matched-audience design**.
- There is **no contribution-margin, CAC, or return-on-ad-spend measure**, even
  though the product is $34 with a 15%-off first order and free two-way
  shipping.
- There is **no repeat-purchase or 60-day retention measure**.
- There is **no refund-, return-rate-, or chargeback measure**, even though the
  policy is 30-day no-questions returns.

## Design and accessibility notes (as written in the plan)

- **Aesthetic:** unpolished, phone-shot, "native to the feed."
- **Alt text:** none is written for any card.
- **Screen-reader / captions:** the plan provides no screen-reader description
  for the still cards and no captions or transcript for the Card 3 video.
- **Meaning conveyed by color:** yes — the recommended option on Card 2 is shown
  only by a green ring (see Card 2).
- **Text density and contrast:** Card 4 sets a large block of overlay text over
  a busy festival photo; some overlay text is light-on-light. The plan gives no
  guidance on minimum text size, contrast, or how much overlay text fits legibly
  on a phone screen.

## Your task

Review this paid-social carousel for its intended audience and tell the team
whether it is likely to work, why, what is weak or unsupported, and what should
change or be tested.
