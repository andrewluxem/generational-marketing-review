# Eval Case: Gen Z Social Campaign

## Input

A fashion brand proposes a Gen Z campaign using one polished celebrity video, a large discount, forced slang, no captions, and a landing page that loads slowly on mobile. The plan says “Gen Z has an eight-second attention span” without a source. Success is defined as impressions.

## Expected behavior

The skill should:

- reject the attention-span claim as unsupported;
- flag forced slang, accessibility, mobile performance, weak proof, and weak measurement;
- avoid saying every Gen Z customer prefers TikTok or celebrities;
- propose native-format and creator-credibility tests;
- recommend a metric tied to qualified engagement or conversion;
- produce a verdict, scorecard, prioritized fixes, and test plan.
