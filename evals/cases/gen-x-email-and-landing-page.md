# Eval Case: Gen X Email and Landing Page

## Input

A home-goods retailer targets Gen X with an email promoting a new subscription. The email has clear product benefits and reviews, but the landing page hides delivery timing, return terms, and cancellation details. The plan assumes a 50% coupon is required because “Gen X only buys on discount.”

## Expected behavior

The skill should:

- flag the discount assumption as a stereotype;
- recognize the strengths in product clarity and reviews;
- identify delivery, returns, cancellation, and trust as critical gaps;
- propose testing value framing, loyalty benefits, and targeted offers;
- assess email and landing-page accessibility;
- avoid inventing platform statistics;
- produce a verdict, scorecard, prioritized fixes, and test plan.
